DUAS & ZIYARAAT

Learn Important Duas & Ziyaraat

Our Duas and Ziyaraat course is designed to help students learn important supplications and Ziyaraat in a simple and structured way.

Students will learn correct pronunciation, memorization and the meanings of selected Duas and Ziyaraat.

What You Will Learn

  • Daily important Duas
  • Selected Ziyaraat
  • Correct pronunciation
  • Memorization techniques
  • Meaning and understanding
  • Regular practice and revision
