YASSARNUL QURAN

Learn Quran Reading From the Basics

Our Yassarnul Quran course is designed for students who want to learn the basics of Quran reading in a simple and effective way.

Students will learn Arabic letters, pronunciation, joining letters, words, and basic Quran reading skills.

What You Will Learn

  • Arabic letters and pronunciation
  • Joining Arabic letters
  • Basic Quran reading
  • Correct pronunciation
  • Regular reading practice
